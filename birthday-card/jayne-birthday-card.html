How to send it

  1. Tap Save the card, then Download this card if you want a fresh copy of the file.
  2. Email: attach the file. On an iPhone/iPad in Mail, tap the attachment → share → Open in Safari (or long-press → Open in New Tab) so the full card plays.
  3. Or send a link: if this card is online, put the link in the email — it opens straight into Safari on iPhone, iPad, Android and desktop.
  4. No fuss option: open it on your screen during a call, and tap through it together.

Little notes

  1. Sound only starts after a tap, so nothing plays by surprise. The speaker button top-right turns it off.
  2. If a browser or email preview blocks interactivity, the card shows every word as a plain page instead — nothing is ever hidden.
  3. “Close the card” at the end clears the edits from this device and returns it to the plain original.
